猫の手も借りたい

ねこのてもかりたい

Want to borrow even a cat's paw

"Extremely busy"

So busy you'd take help from anyone.
年末は忙しくて、猫の手も借りたい状況だ。 (I'm so busy at the end of the year, I'd take any help I can get.)

サバを読む

さばをよむ

Reading the mackerel

"To fudge numbers"

Manipulating counts or age to one's advantage.
彼は年齢を２歳ほどサバを読んでいる。 (He is fudging his age by about two years.)

頭が固い

あたまがかたい

Hard head

"Stubborn / Inflexible"

Unwilling to change ideas or ways.
彼は頭が固くて、新しい方法を認めようとしない。 (He is so stubborn that he won't accept new methods.)

やきもちを焼く

やきもちをやく

Grilling mochi

"To be jealous"

Feeling romantic jealousy.
彼女が他の人と楽しそうに話していると、彼はやきもちを焼く。 (He gets jealous when she talks happily with others.)

耳が痛い

みみがいたい

Ears are aching

"Hard to hear the truth"

Finding a direct criticism painful to hear.
生活習慣を注意されて、耳が痛い。 (It's hard to hear the truth when someone warns me about my lifestyle.)

水に流す

みずにながす

Let it flow into the water

"Forgive and forget"

To wash away past grudges.
喧嘩のことは水に流して、仲直りしよう。 (Let's forgive and forget the fight and make up.)

朝飯前

あさめしまえ

Before breakfast

"Piece of cake"

Something very easy to finish.
この程度の修理なら朝飯前だよ。 (A simple repair like this is a piece of cake.)

骨を折る

ほねをおる

Breaking a bone

"Take great pains"

To work very hard on something.
交渉のためにずいぶんと骨を折った。 (I took great pains to handle the negotiations.)

油を売る

あぶらをうる

Selling oil

"To idle away time"

Spending time chatting instead of working.
仕事の途中で油を売っていて、怒られた。 (I got scolded for idling away my time during work.)

根回し

ねまわし

Digging around the roots

"Laying groundwork"

Building informal consensus beforehand.
会議の前に根回しをしておいた。 (I laid the groundwork before the meeting.)

顔が広い

かおがひろい

Wide face

"Well-connected"

Knowing many influential people.
彼は政界に顔が広い。 (He is well-connected in the political world.)

猫をかぶる

ねこをかぶる

Wearing a cat

"Playing innocent"

Feigning gentleness or ignorance.
彼は家では荒いが、外では猫をかぶっている。 (He is rough at home but plays innocent in public.)

猿も木から落ちる

さるもきからおちる

Even monkeys fall from trees

"Even experts fail"

Anyone, no matter how skilled, can make a mistake.
名医がまさかのミス。猿も木から落ちるね。 (Even a great doctor made a mistake. Anyone can fail.)

鰻登り

うなぎのぼり

Climbing like an eel

"Skyrocketing"

Rising rapidly and steeply.
新商品の売れ行きが鰻登りだ。 (The sales of the new product are skyrocketing.)

炎上する

えんじょうする

Going up in flames

"Social media backlash"

Huge criticism or controversy online.
失言がネットで炎上した。 (The slip of the tongue caused a huge backlash online.)

芽が出る

めがでる

Sprouting a bud

"Showing signs of success"

Efforts finally starting to show results.
下積み時代を経て、ようやく芽が出た。 (After years of struggling, I'm finally seeing success.)

気が短い

きがみじかい

Short energy

"Short-tempered"

Losing patience quickly.
彼は気が短いので、すぐ怒る。 (He is short-tempered, so he gets angry easily.)

花より団子

はなよりだんご

Dumplings over flowers

"Practicality over beauty"

Choosing substance over aesthetics.
景色よりも食事。まさに花より団子だ。 (Food over views. It's truly dumplings over flowers.)

伏線回収

ふくせんかいしゅう

Collecting hidden lines

"Payoff of foreshadowing"

Earlier hints finally being explained.
最終回での伏線回収に感動した。 (I was moved by how the foreshadowing paid off in the finale.)

鵜呑みにする

うのみにする

Swallow like a cormorant

"Accept without question"

Believing everything without thinking.
ニュースを鵜呑みにするのは危険だ。 (It's dangerous to accept news without question.)

ネタバレ

ねたばれ

Material leak

"Spoiler"

Revealing the plot of a story.
映画の結末をネタバレされた。 (The ending of the movie was spoiled for me.)

元を取る

もとをとる

Taking the origin

"Get money's worth"

To recoup the costs spent.
食べ放題で元を取ろうと頑張る。 (I'll try my best to get my money's worth at the buffet.)

ぼったくり

ぼったくり

Plucking/Stripping

"Rip-off"

Unfairly high prices charged to customers.
この店はぼったくりだから気をつけろ。 (Be careful, this shop is a rip-off.)

バズる

ばずる

Creating a buzz

"To go viral"

For content on the internet to spread rapidly.
彼女がSNSに投稿した猫の動画が瞬く間にバズった。 (The cat video she posted on social media went viral in no time.)

空気を読む

くうきをよむ

Reading the air

"Reading the room"

Sensing the mood of a situation and acting accordingly, without being told.
彼は空気を読んで、話題を変えた。 (He read the room and changed the subject.)

丸投げ

まるなげ

Throwing the whole thing

"Dumping all the work"

Handing over an entire task to someone else without doing any of it yourself.
彼は仕事を部下に丸投げした。 (He dumped the whole job on his subordinate.)

ドタキャン

どたきゃん

Last-minute cancel

"To bail at the last minute"

Cancelling suddenly, right before an appointment or plan.
デートをドタキャンされて、がっかりした。 (I was disappointed when my date bailed on me at the last minute.)

推し

おし

The one you push

"My fave"

The idol, character, or person you passionately support as a fan.
私の推しは、このグループのボーカルだ。 (My fave is the vocalist of this group.)

目から鱗

めからうろこ

Scales fall from my eyes

"Eye-opening realization"

Suddenly understanding something clearly for the first time.
💡 Note: English has the same image: "the scales fell from my eyes" (from the Bible). Some expressions match perfectly!
先生の説明を聞いて、目から鱗だった。 (Hearing the teacher's explanation was a real eye-opener.)

喉から手が出る

のどからてがでる

A hand comes out of my throat

"Want it desperately"

Wanting something so badly that you can almost reach out and grab it.
限定モデルが、喉から手が出るほど欲しい。 (I want the limited edition model so badly.)

足を引っ張る

あしをひっぱる

Pulling someone's leg

"Holding someone back"

Hindering someone's progress or success, or being a burden on a team.
💡 Note: Careful! In English, "pulling your leg" means joking or teasing. Same words, totally different meaning!
彼のミスが、チームの足を引っ張った。 (His mistakes dragged the team down.)

腹黒い

はらぐろい

Black belly

"Scheming / Two-faced"

Sweet on the outside, but secretly plotting something.
彼は笑顔だが、実は腹黒い。 (He smiles, but he's actually quite scheming.)

口が軽い

くちがかるい

Light mouth

"Blabbermouth"

Someone who cannot keep secrets and talks too easily.
彼女は口が軽いから、秘密は話さない方がいい。 (She's a blabbermouth, so don't tell her any secrets.)

鼻が高い

はながたかい

High nose

"Beaming with pride"

Feeling very proud, especially of someone close to you.
息子が優勝して、鼻が高い。 (My son won the championship, and I'm beaming with pride.)

猫に小判

ねこにこばん

Gold coins to a cat

"Wasted on the unappreciative"

Giving something valuable to someone who cannot appreciate its worth.
💡 Note: English says "pearls before swine" — a different animal, but the same idea!
高いワインをあげたが、彼には猫に小判だった。 (I gave him expensive wine, but it was wasted on him.)

七転び八起き

ななころびやおき

Fall seven times, rise eight

"Never give up"

No matter how many times you fail, you get back up and try again.
七転び八起きの精神で、挑戦し続ける。 (With a never-give-up spirit, I keep on trying.)

石の上にも三年

いしのうえにもさんねん

Three years on a stone

"Perseverance pays off"

Even a cold stone warms up if you sit on it for three years — patience brings results.
石の上にも三年で、続けることが大切だ。 (Perseverance is key — good things come to those who persist.)

ちりも積もれば山となる

ちりもつもればやまとなる

Dust piles up into a mountain

"Small efforts add up"

Tiny efforts, repeated many times, eventually become something big.
毎日少しずつ貯金すれば、ちりも積もれば山となる。 (Save a little every day, and it will add up to a lot.)

井の中の蛙

いのなかのかわず

A frog in a well

"Narrow-minded"

Knowing only your own small world, and thinking it is everything.
彼は井の中の蛙で、他のやり方を知らない。 (He's a frog in a well — he knows no other way of doing things.)

芋を洗うよう

いもをあらうよう

Like washing potatoes

"Packed like sardines"

So crowded that people are pressed together, like potatoes being washed in a tub.
朝の電車は、芋を洗うような混雑だ。 (The morning train is packed like sardines.)
